# Egypt and selectable international HVAC standards: evidence audit

Research date: 2026-10-08. Scope: the approved professional CAD/HVAC design specification, `standards/designStandards.ts`, `knowledgeBase.ts`, `validation/nfpa90aValidator.ts`, and its NFPA database in `hvacCatalogs.ts`. This is a source registry and implementation gap assessment, not a compliance certificate. No engine files were edited.

## Publicly supportable calculation and classification evidence

The ASH-VRP-HIST publisher PDF supports the method `Vbz = Rp * Pz + Ra * Az`: use occupancy evidence for population `Pz`, net occupiable zone area `Az`, a sourced per-person rate `Rp`, and a sourced per-area rate `Ra`. It also distinguishes `Voz = Vbz / Ez` and system intake `Vot`. Therefore a breathing-zone sum is not sufficient evidence for the outdoor-air intake of a multizone recirculating system. Keep unit systems consistent and expose intermediate terms. Example with explicitly supplied project inputs (not an asserted normative table): 5 cfm/person × 10 people + 0.06 cfm/ft² × 1,000 ft² = 110 cfm breathing-zone airflow; `Ez = 0.8` yields 137.5 cfm zone outdoor airflow. Rate/table provenance and complete selected-edition method remain necessary.

ASH-DUCT-2024 assigns the 14 ft limitation to **air connectors** and describes rated-wall/floor restrictions, listing and manufacturer instructions. It separately identifies 6 ft, fully stretched, as a commercial flexible-duct Handbook recommendation. These categories must remain separate. This is sufficient evidence to reject a universal NFPA “all flexible air ducts <= 14 ft” claim. It is not evidence to approve arbitrary duct length or to substitute the Handbook recommendation for a legal code limit. NFPA-HIST-PROCESS corroborates the connector classification historically.

No final official NFPA detector clause was extractable during this audit. The existing 2,000/15,000 cfm numbers should be retained only as unverified legacy reference checks until the chosen final edition is reviewed. Do not correct section numbers or implement exemption conditions from unofficial copies. Record unresolved questions explicitly: supply detector location relative to filters/branches; each-story common-return arrangement; complete area-detection protection exceptions; exhaust-only units; smoke-control mode; fan shutdown/alarm interface; and final edition renumbering.

## Recommended Egypt project profile

Keep jurisdiction and reference selections independent:

- `jurisdiction`: country `EG`, city/governorate, permitting/fire authority (unknown until identified), project occupancy/use, building height/stories, new/existing scope, site conditions and project phase.
- `localRequirements[]`: official code title/number, volume, edition and amendments; legal/adoption instrument URL or controlled project document; applicable scope; status `unknown`, `documented`, `reviewed`; reviewing engineer/authority and date. The default Egypt profile contains unresolved local requirements, not fabricated code editions.
- `references[]`: publisher, exact identifier/title, edition, unit system, addenda/errata/TIA set, source URL/document hash, access basis, content kind (`normative`, `handbook-guidance`, `publisher-metadata`, `project-specification`), role (`contract-reference`, `adopted-local-reference`, `advisory`), applicability basis and reviewer. An international standard becomes an adopted local reference only with adoption evidence.
- `rules[]`: reference ID, exact section/table/notes, parameter values/units, applicability predicates and exceptions, required evidence, implementation coverage and analytical validation case. Metadata verification and normative-text verification are separate states.
- `overrides[]`: rule/parameter, project value/units, justification/source, author/date/review and disposition of conflicts with local requirements. An override does not waive legal requirements automatically.
- `results[]`: evaluated/not-applicable/unknown/failed with inputs, sources, assumptions, missing evidence and responsible reviewer. Report checks actually performed; overall issue readiness requires resolved applicable local requirements and evidence. Never label the profile or export “Egypt compliant” from international-reference selection alone.

Immediate release-safe boundary: retain calculations and presets as preliminary design assistance, implement provenance/applicability and explicit unknown states, and obtain controlled official texts before asserting normative compliance. Licensed/read-only access permits reviewing a standard; reproduction or embedding rights must be checked separately. No Egyptian international adoption or certification was established here.
